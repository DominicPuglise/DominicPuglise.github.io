Top 3 Favorite Classes

My most enjoyable and impactful classes so far:

  • Computer-Aided Design of Machines
        This course focused on the fundamentals of pure mechanism design, both manually and through computer-aided methods. I found it particularly eye-opening as it showcased the elegance of mechanical solutions, especially in contrast to the growing reliance on electrical actuators as a quick fix.
  • Fluid Mechanics
        Building on the principles of Thermodynamics, this course deepened my appreciation for the mathematical and analytical methods used to describe complex motion. I plan to take additional courses in this field if possible, and I have already noticed unexpected overlaps with later subjects like Heat Transfer, which has further strengthened my interest in fluid dynamics.
  • Mechanics I-III
        As a foundational series for multiple engineering disciplines, these courses were essential to my understanding of mechanical principles. Taking them in my first year of engineering school reaffirmed my choice of major and provided a strong introduction to the field of mechanical engineering.
